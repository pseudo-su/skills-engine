# Principles

- Ground skills in concrete work, user corrections, and tested judgement.
- A fresh execution tests whether a skill carries its understanding beyond the authoring conversation.
- Treat revisions as improvement hypotheses; adoption alone is not evidence of improvement.
- Keep observations, interpretations, proposals, and agreed decisions distinguishable.
- Prefer cohesive methods over aggressive deduplication; composition must preserve information flow and constraints.
- Separate the method from interaction policy. Accept choices already made rather than repeating the path that established them.
- Recast preserves requirements and learned judgement while reconsidering implementation. Readiness, blind writing, evaluation, and incidental diff minimisation are part of its contract.
- Isolation is selective and operational: retain necessary inputs while excluding evidence that would anchor the independent task.
- Extract only useful outcomes, into an appropriate durable home. No extraction is a valid result.
- Personal and team improvement share a loop; ownership, review, and local scope differ.
- Preserve a canonical version-controlled source and the ancestry of public imports.

Names, packaging, and composition syntax remain provisional until use tests them.
